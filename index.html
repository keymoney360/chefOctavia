A little about the chef

Passion on the plate.
Care in every detail.

Chef Octavia is a professional chef and catering specialist based in Nyeri, Kenya, with experience working in leading five-star hotels across Kenya, the UAE and Sri Lanka

With a diverse culinary background and extensive knowledge of Mediterranean, Middle Eastern, British, Asian, American and Pan-African cuisines, Chef Octavia creates thoughtfully crafted menus tailored to each client and occasion.

From intimate private dinners and birthdays to graduations, weddings, corporate events and large celebrations, every menu is prepared with quality ingredients, homemade flavors and beautiful presentation.

Whether you are looking for a private chef experience, customized catering or a memorable dining experience, Chef Octavia brings professional hospitality standards, creativity and a passion for exceptional food to every event.
